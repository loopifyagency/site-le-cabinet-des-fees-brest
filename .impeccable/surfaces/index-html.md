# Surface brief: index.html (Le Cabinet des Fées - Brest)

Mode: Persuade. Single-page site, French, static HTML/CSS/JS.

Audience, job, action: Brest residents and passers-through, often on a phone, deciding in seconds whether this is the right place and then calling 02 98 46 83 06. Proof: five real client reviews reproduced verbatim, the address, the hours, two real photographs (195 x 139 px sources, so shown small). Constraints: palette #4F5A34 #D9CFA5 #FCF5EC; naturel, féérique, magique; GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis vendored; no invented prices, durations, numbers or claims; booking by phone only; every section differs in layout and motion.

Chosen direction and memorable moment: "L'atelier de la fée marraine". Wellbeing as hand-sewn care. A single golden thread (le fil d'or) is the red thread of the page: it is drawn in the loader, unspools through the hero, becomes the vertical stitched timeline of the ritual, the running-stitch rail of the horizontal treatments, the line threading through four sewn buttons, and finally passes through the call button. Memorable moment: the cream loader unzips along a pinked seam to reveal the moss-felt hero while the thread keeps drawing.

Unresolved decisions: social links, e-mail, regular Sunday hours, treatments list and prices, years of activity, more photographs, legal notice. Roll was degraded (no challengers); assignment 7 of 7 honoured because it fits every pinned constraint.

## Direction contract

THESIS: This page is a fairy godmother's atelier where care is cut and sewn to measure; one golden thread links every section to the phone call. It refuses the category default: eucalyptus-and-arch spa template with a serif and stock photography.

OWN-WORLD: Felt, linen and tissue paper. Moss felt #4F5A34 and a deeper moss lining as the big fields, sand linen #D9CFA5 for ribbons and patches, cream tissue #FCF5EC with dashed cut-lines and notches as the reading ground, gold thread for stitches, fireflies and light. Pinked (zigzag) seams between sections, appliqué leaf-wings with running stitches, buttons with holes, cross-stitch. Gloock display, Ballet script only for single gold words, Hanken Grotesk text. Recognisable with every word removed.

STORY: The visitor understands in seconds that this is a gentle, hand-made place in the heart of Brest; believes it through five unedited reviews and a real address; and calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen moss felt. Three-line Gloock headline "Une / parenthèse / enchantée" set at the scale of the viewport, the first line sharing its row with the introduction text, the third carrying a tilted photo patch with stitched edge. Three parallax foliage layers, fireflies, and the gold thread unspooling from the top-left corner along the headline down to the primary button "Prendre rendez-vous" at the bottom left. Open-today status bottom right. Nav top, hides on scroll down.

FORM: Candidate 7 of 7 on my ordered list (L'atelier de la fée marraine: couture, patron, boutons, courtepointe), seed: 20bec12e4c49e53d.
