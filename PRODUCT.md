# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, confirmed by the user (no framework, no build step). GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Lenis are named in the brief; they ship vendored inside the repository rather than from a CDN. Deploy target not constrained beyond "hosted from the GitHub repository" (any static host works).

## Users

Residents of Brest and its surroundings (and visitors passing through) looking for a massage, a beauty treatment or simply an hour of calm. They arrive on a phone as often as on a desktop, usually with one question: is this the right place, and how do I book. The job: decide quickly, then call to book. The visitor's language is French.

## Product Purpose

Le Cabinet des Fées - Brest is an institut de beauté presented as a "maison du bien-être" at 15 Rue des 11 Martyrs, 29200 Brest. The website exists to turn a curious visitor into a booked appointment. The one action that counts: prendre rendez-vous, which today means calling 02 98 46 83 06. Success is a phone call.

## Positioning

A "parenthèse enchantée" in the heart of Brest: a fairy-tale, soothing setting where each treatment is a moment out of time, built on gentleness, listening and kindness, with a refined and intimate experience. The approach is global well-being engaging body, mind and energy. The people behind it are named by clients as Stéphanie and Marc.

## Operating Context

- Address: 15 Rue des 11 Martyrs, 29200 Brest. Google Maps short link: https://maps.app.goo.gl/YWWg7HZTTjS1H1FR9
- Phone: 02 98 46 83 06 (landline: no SMS).
- Hours given: Monday to Wednesday 09:00-19:30; Thursday to Saturday 09:00-20:30; "dimanche (Toussaint)" 09:00-19:30, which is a special-date line, not a confirmed regular Sunday opening. "Les horaires peuvent être modifiés."
- Booking: by phone only. No online booking tool, no e-mail address, no social-media accounts were provided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Booking is phone-only; every "book" action is a `tel:` link. No form backend exists.
- The services list was "à compléter". It is authored from the positioning and the client reviews (massages are confirmed by reviews; the other treatment families are inferred from "institut de beauté") with NO prices, NO durations, NO product names. The owner must confirm or correct it.
- Undecided / unknown: regular Sunday hours, social-media URLs, e-mail address, years of activity, number of clients, certifications, prices, online booking link.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Le Cabinet des Fées - Brest".
- Mood stated by the owner: naturel, féérique, magique.
- Imposed colours: #4F5A34, #D9CFA5, #FCF5EC.
- Language: French. Tone: alive and concrete, never generic.
- Copy provided by the owner (kept as source of truth): "Une Parenthèse Enchantée", the value statement ("bien-être global qui engage le corps, l'esprit et l'énergie ... douceur, écoute et bienveillance ... expérience raffinée et intime") and the closing prompt "Prêt à vous évader ? Réservez votre moment de détente".

## Evidence on Hand

- Five real Google reviews, to be reproduced word for word and never invented or altered: Elodie Lnt, Justine Pessel, Poli hgcg, Emmie dsct, Christine Kerdraon.
- Two real photographs of low resolution (about 195 x 140 px): the shop front (`assets/img/facade.jpg`) and the interior with the patchwork throw (`assets/img/interieur.jpg`).
- Absent and not to be fabricated: star rating or review count, prices, years of experience, client counts, portraits of the team, additional photographs, a hero video.

## Product Principles

1. The phone call is the finish line: every section ends by making it easier to call.
2. Real words from real clients carry the proof; the site never claims what it cannot show.
3. Softness is the product: nothing aggressive, nothing hurried, even when the motion is spectacular.
4. Say only what is true about the place; where content is missing, mark it for the owner instead of inventing it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sufficient contrast, descriptive alt text, full keyboard navigation, and a lighter experience for `prefers-reduced-motion`. The main audience includes people who may be tired, stressed or reading on a small phone in poor light.
